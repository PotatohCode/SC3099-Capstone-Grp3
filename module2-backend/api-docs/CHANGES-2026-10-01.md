## Module 3 — Face Recognition

PR #7 (`03adb9f`) finished 7 tasks; they're listed under
[Completed tasks](CHANGELOG.md#completed-team-tasks) in the changelog.

| Priority | Task | How | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Should** (improved) | Handle ~100 concurrent calls | PR #7 measured in the Docker container (2 workers): ~13–14 image requests/s, so about **30–35 simultaneous check-ins** with a photo finish inside the backend's 5 s, and **~45–50 bursts at once** inside the 15 s burst timeout. Beyond that, calls time out and those check-ins are **flagged** (not rejected) while the backend pauses calls for 30 s. A 3rd worker would fit in memory (~+400–650 MB each, container limit 3.5 GB) | [Face: how we call you](API-FOR-FACE-RECOGNITION.md#how-we-call-you), M3 `CHANGES.md` 2026-10-24 (6) |
| FYI | A low score on the front-facing frame makes a burst `unverified` | For a burst you return `liveness_passed: null` (never `false`) when the passive score is under 0.6, and `challenge_passed: true` + `liveness_passed: null` is unverified on our side (no attempt used, at most flagged). That's the intended safe side; if it turns out to happen often on real webcams, tell us | [Face §3](API-FOR-FACE-RECOGNITION.md#3-post-livenesscheck) |
| FYI | Keep `/face/enroll` returning `201` + `enrollment_successful: false` | The backend turns it into `400 NO_FACE_DETECTED`; a 4xx would show students "service unavailable" | [Face §1](API-FOR-FACE-RECOGNITION.md#1-post-faceenroll) |
